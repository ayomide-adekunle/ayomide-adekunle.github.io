# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2, 2025_

## Introduction

**BabyX** (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

---

## Information We Collect

- **Personal Data:** We do **not** collect or store any personal data on our servers.
- **Photos & Videos:** All photos and videos you capture or upload are stored **locally on your device** and, if you choose, in your personal Google Drive.
- **Google Account:** If you choose to sync with Google Drive, we request access to your Google account for authentication and storage purposes only.

---

## How We Use Your Information

- To enable you to capture, organize, and view your baby’s photos and videos.
- To allow you to back up and restore your data using your own Google Drive account.
- To provide you with entertainment features, such as funny baby videos.

---

## Data Security

- Your data is stored **locally on your device** and/or **in your personal Google Drive**.
- We do **not** have access to your photos, videos, or personal information.
- We do **not** share your data with third parties.

---

## Google Drive

- If you choose to use Google Drive sync, the app will request permission to access your Google Drive for backup and restore purposes.
- All data stored in Google Drive is under your control and can be deleted by you at any time.

---

## Children’s Privacy

BabyX is designed for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
